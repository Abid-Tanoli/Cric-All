## 2. Phone par jo dekha (375×812)
- **Cricinfo** `/live-cricket-score`: upar 3 tabs (Live, Upcoming, Result), **neeche 5 bottom buttons tasveer + lafz**, har match ek saaf card.
- **PlayCricket**: ek pehgham, ek peela button.
- **CricAll** Home: hamburger menu, featured series, search, live, results; asal kaam (match shuru) kahin nahi.

Cricbuzz browser tool mein band tha; CricHeroes FAQ aur ECB help ne automatic parhne se roka (403), is liye un ki baatein search ke khulase se hain.

## 4. Kya na lein
- Match se pehle pitch type, ground, ball, sab poochna (CricHeroes): sab "baad mein".
- Scorer ka alag registration aur 48 ghante intezar (CricClubs, 2019 guide).
- Ads scoring ke dauran (Android Authority ne CricHeroes ke ads ko tang karne wala kaha).
- Chhote touch targets (Cricbuzz ke ek app review mein shikayat).

## 5. Hamari alag pehchan
1. **International standard app + forms mein Urdu madad**: in zarayon mein kisi competitor ka Urdu madad wala dawa nahi mila (poori tehqeeq nahi).
2. **Har idara**: school, college, university, company, NGO ek hi jagah.
3. Muft tournament hub, sponsors, OBS overlay.
4. Pakistan ka province → tehsil location.

## 6. Jo pakka nahi
- CricHeroes offline: GullyCrix kehta hai internet chahiye, CricPulse "partial"; dono competitor hain.
- Prices mulk aur store ke hisaab se badalti hain.
- Play-Cricket ki scoring screen ke taps nahi mile.

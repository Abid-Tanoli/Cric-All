# Lafz: English app, forms mein Urdu madad

**Faisla (malik, 25 Nov 2026):** app international standard, English. Urdu **sirf forms waghera** mein: form fields, tasdeeq, ghalti, net ki halat, help.
**Urdu kisi Urdu parhne wale aur ek local scorer se tasdeeq karwani baqi hai.**

## Qaide
- English asaan ho, 6 lafz tak. Cricket ke international lafz: Wide, No ball, LBW, Run out.
- Button = kaam ("Start a match"), naam nahi ("Match Setup Wizard").
- Screen par yeh lafz **mana**: Roster, Validation, Self-service, Wizard, Session, Placeholder.
- Urdu line chhoti, English ke **neeche**, English ki jagah kabhi nahi.
- Hindse 0–9.
